android-overlay — hand-written Android sources, kept safe from regeneration

`src-tauri/gen/android/` is gitignored and is regenerated by
`cargo tauri android init`. These two files are NOT generated — they were
written by hand and carry the share-target feature:

  app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
      adds the ACTION_SEND intent-filter, so anything shareable on the phone
      (a link from Chrome, text from anywhere) can be sent to Renaissance Man.

  app/src/main/java/com/renaissanceman/app/MainActivity.kt
      receives that intent and pushes {id,text} onto window.__RM_SHARES, then
      calls window.RM_drainShares() (see frontend/js/app/vault.js) which saves
      it into Journal/inbox/ via the existing save_capture command.

If gen/android is ever regenerated, copy these two files back over the
generated ones before building, or the share door disappears silently —
the app still builds and runs, it just stops appearing in the share sheet.

Mirror path:
  android-overlay/app/src/main/...  ->  src-tauri/gen/android/app/src/main/...
