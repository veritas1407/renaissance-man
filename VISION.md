## 1. What this is

**Renaissance Man is an intellectual operating system for becoming.**

It exists for one kind of person: the ambitious, multidisciplinary mind — the person who is always in the middle of five books, three obsessions, one long question, and a body they are training — and who wants those things to *compound over a lifetime* instead of evaporating between enthusiasms.

It is not a notes app, a task manager, a read-later queue, or a second brain. Excellent products exist for storing knowledge. This is not one of them, and it must never compete with them, because it is playing a different game:

- A vault **stores** knowledge. Renaissance Man **cultivates** it.
- A PKM **organizes** notes. Renaissance Man **develops understanding**.
- A productivity app **tracks** output. Renaissance Man **guides becoming**.

The one-line answer: *a personal university, mentor, observatory, library, and workshop — fused into a single quiet place that remembers who you are trying to become, and makes every return to that work free.*

## 2. The primitive: becoming

Everything in the system exists at one of three altitudes, and the altitudes explain every feature:

```
BECOMING      the pursuit — who you are turning into
   ↓ generates
INQUIRY       threads — the living questions the pursuit runs on
   ↓ gathers
MATERIAL      readings · notes · highlights · rungs · workouts · works
```

- **Material** without inquiry is a warehouse. (This is what every PKM builds.)
- **Inquiry** without becoming is trivia. (This is what curiosity alone produces.)
- **Becoming** is the reason both exist: *become an AI product leader; understand physics deeply; master the body; write books.* Every pursuit naturally sheds questions, books, projects, skills, experiments, and works — the system's job is to catch all of it, connect it, and hand it back at the right moment, so the pursuit survives every gap in attention.

The user never "builds a knowledge system." They pursue. The system assembles itself around the pursuit.

## 3. The three laws

Descended from years of design work; unchanged in spirit, restated at the right altitude.

**First Law — Re-entry is free.**
The owner's mind rotates between obsessions. That rotation is not the disease; it is the polymath engine (Leonardo never stayed put — his notebooks made his pivots lossless). The system therefore never resists a pivot; it *harvests* it. Everything dropped is sealed with its context; everything sealed returns with its context. Out of sight must never mean gone.

**Second Law — The user does the becoming.**
Producing is how humans keep what they learn. The system may find, connect, schedule, propose, and ask — it may never read for you, summarize for you, synthesize for you, or hold positions for you. Any feature where the machine does the articulating automates the becoming away, and is banned by law, not by taste.

**Third Law — The system survives neglect.**
No streaks, no shame, no badges, no nags, no red "you failed" states. Come back after a month and the room says *here is where we stood*, never *you were gone*. Attention is the scarcest resource in the system: at most one ask per session, and quiet is the default. Empty is a feature.

## 4. What it is not

- **Not a chatbot.** Conversation is answer-shaped; this system is question-shaped. The resident speaks in a scribe's line or two, never a chat pane.
- **Not a productivity tool.** It measures movement of a mind (positions changed, works produced, returns made), never busyness.
- **Not a social product.** One owner, one vault, no feed, no comparison. "The feed is closed" is written on the hearth.
- **Not a game.** Progress is made legible and ceremonial — a lit star, a pressed seal — but never incentivized with points, streaks, or variable rewards. Real accomplishment is the only currency.
- **Not themed.** The palace (library at midnight, observatory, workshop) is a feeling, never a costume. No crests, no spells, no cosplay.

## 5. The North Star metric

**Compounding.** Concretely, three measures no filing system can fake:

1. **Re-entry rate** — of the things the owner dropped, how many did they return to? (The First Law, as a number.)
2. **Positions changed per season** — evidence of a mind actually moving, recorded in dated position updates.
3. **Works per season** — evidence the mind produces, not only consumes.

Daily active use is explicitly *not* a goal. A month of absence followed by one free re-entry is a success story.

## 6. The thirty-year test

Every decision must pass: *will this still be meaningful when the owner is old?*

The vault is plain Markdown in folders the owner controls — readable in any editor, in any decade, after every app including this one is dead. The sky of named constellations, the shelf of works, the dated record of every position held and revised — these are being written for a reader thirty years away: the owner, looking back at the shape of a mind that kept asking.

The goal was never software. It is the notebook Leonardo would have built — the one that made his rotations lossless, his returns free, and his many obsessions one continuous act of becoming.
